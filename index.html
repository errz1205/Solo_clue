📋 How to Play

  • Goal: Deduce which Suspect, Weapon, and Room are in the secret envelope
  • Your Turn: Roll dice, click highlighted cells to move (green = reachable)
  • Enter a Room: Make a Suggestion to learn information from AI opponents
  • Watch AI Turns: Observe their suggestions and who refutes them — this reveals clues!
  • Accuse: When you're certain, make your final Accusation (but be careful — wrong = game over!)
  • Notepad: Click cells to mark X (impossible), O (confirmed), or ? (maybe)
